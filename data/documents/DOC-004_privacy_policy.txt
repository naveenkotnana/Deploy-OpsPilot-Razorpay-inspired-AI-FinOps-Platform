1. ANONYMISED IDENTIFIERS ONLY
The platform stores anonymised surrogate identifiers only. Apartment, building,
and device identifiers are surrogate codes. No customer name, phone number,
email address, or postal address is stored or processed.

2. NO PERSONAL DATA
No personally identifiable information enters the pipeline at any stage,
including logs, audit records, and LLM prompts.

3. LOCAL INFERENCE
All language model inference runs locally through Ollama. No data is sent to an
external or paid language model API. Retrieval, embedding, and generation all
execute on the local host.

4. SYNTHETIC DATA
All data in this environment is synthetic and generated for demonstration. It
does not represent real customers, real revenue, or real operational events.

5. LOG CONTENT
Structured logs record identifiers, workflow ids, and trace ids. Tool inputs are
recorded as a hash rather than raw content in the audit log.

6. ACCESS LEVELS
Documents carry an access_level of PUBLIC, INTERNAL, or RESTRICTED. Retrieval
filters documents by the requesting role's clearance. A role may never retrieve
a document above its clearance.
