1. SCOPE
This procedure defines how monthly subscription revenue is calculated for
apartment water metering and device rental services. It applies to every
billing month close.

2. WATER REVENUE
Water revenue = prorated base fee + measured consumption x approved rate.
The base fee is prorated by active days: base_fee * (water_active_days /
days_in_billing_month). Consumption is the sum of daily consumption_m3 readings
for the water device within the billing month, multiplied by the plan's
rate_per_m3. Consumption is never estimated or extrapolated.

3. RENTAL REVENUE
Rental revenue = monthly rental x active days / billing-month days. The rental
device must have an activation date on or before the month end. A device
deactivated mid-month is billed only for days up to and including the
deactivation date.

4. TOTAL REVENUE
Total apartment revenue = water revenue + rental revenue. Location revenue is
the sum of apartment revenue for that location code. Plan revenue is grouped by
the plan assigned to the device during the billing month.

5. ACTIVE DAYS
Active days are the overlap between the device active window and the billing
month, inclusive of both endpoints. A device activated on the 15th of a 30 day
month has 16 active days. A device with zero overlap has zero active days and
contributes zero revenue.

6. MISSING INPUTS
Never guess missing financial inputs. If a plan, rate, or device record
required for a calculation is absent, the apartment row is written with
validation_flag EXCEPTION and an exception_code identifying the missing input.
Revenue for the affected component is recorded as zero and the row is routed to
the manual validation queue. Imputing a value is prohibited.

7. ROUNDING
Monetary amounts are rounded half-up to two decimal places at the point the
component is computed, not at aggregation time.

8. RECALCULATION
Revenue for a billing month is fully recomputed from source records. Rows are
never hand-edited. A recalculation replaces all rows for that billing month.
